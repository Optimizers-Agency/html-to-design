Keep the page visible, then press Capture.

Then click the Figma canvas and press Ctrl+V. Every text, image and shape arrives as its own layer.

Capture notes
    How do I get this into Figma?
    1. Paste (fastest): press Copy for Figma, click the Figma canvas, press Ctrl+V.
    2. SVG file: download the .svg and drag it onto the canvas.
    3. Plugin (best text): the .layerlift.json file only opens through the bundled Layerlift Import plugin in the Figma desktop app. Figma cannot open it by drag-and-drop.
    4. Got one flat image instead of layers? The file was too heavy for Figma. Keep Images on Compact, or capture the page in parts with "Visible area" or "One element".
